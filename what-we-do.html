What your ally does

Four ways we help your business. Start with one. Add more as you grow.

Tell us about your business

Sell

Never miss a customer again.

Your ally answers customer questions, recommends the right products, books appointments and takes orders, day or night. Customer no go wait again.

How it looks

Shop: a customer wants a perfume for evenings under ₦20,000. Your ally suggests three, with prices.

Farm: a buyer asks your price per kilo and orders from your next harvest.

Service: a client asks how much braids cost and books a Saturday 10am slot.

It only suggests what you actually sell, and passes anything important to you, like confirming stock or payment.

Track

Know your numbers without guessing.

Simple records for sales, stock, costs, clients and payments. Works on your phone and your laptop. No more "I think say e remain small."

How it looks

Shop: see which items are running low before they finish.

Farm: every pond in one place: batch, fish count, feed, costs and losses.

Service: who paid, who owes, and which clients haven't come back in three months.

Check

Quality checks without the long hours.

Photos and data your team checks by hand, checked for you. Your people only look at what needs a human eye.

How it looks

Research firms: quality checks (QC) on field submissions. Photos sorted and checked automatically, with only the doubtful ones sent to a person.

Shop: product photos checked against your catalogue.

Farm: daily records checked for missing entries before they become a problem.

Advise

Know your next move.

We look through your records with you and tell you what we see: what's growing, what's slipping, and what needs your attention.

How it looks

Shop: which products bring the most money, and which take the most effort for little return.

Farm: more fish dying in one pond than the others? Time to call the vet.

Service: your busiest days, so you know when to have more hands on deck.

Coming next: regular reports from your ally, so you can ask "How did last week go?" anytime.
