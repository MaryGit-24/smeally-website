Why SME Ally exists

I grew up helping in my parents' businesses: the stock, the customers, the records, and the work that never ends. Later I ran my own, and saw the same struggle from the inside.

Big companies have teams and systems for this. Most SMEs have the owner and a few hands. SME Ally is here to change that.

SMEs are the backbone of Africa. Dem deserve better tools.
