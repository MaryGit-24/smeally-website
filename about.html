Why SME Ally exists

My parents ran different businesses while I was growing up, and I was right in the middle of most of them, helping out. That's where I learned how a business really runs: the stock, the customers, the records, and how much of the day goes to work that never ends. Later I started a business of my own, and I saw the same struggle from the inside.

Big companies have teams and systems for all of this. Most SMEs have the owner and a few hands. I started SME Ally to change that, and to give every SME an ally that helps with the daily load and helps them make better decisions.

SMEs are the backbone of Africa. Dem deserve better tools.

How it started

SME Ally began as a project supported by the Nigeria AI Collective and Data Science Nigeria. My first build was for Elixir Haven, a fragrance business: a sales assistant that knows every product, recommends the right scent, and helps close the sale. Since then I've built a record system for a catfish farm, and I'm now building automatic quality checks for survey research.

Every tool started the same way: sitting with the business, understanding the real problem, and building something that saves real hours.

None of this has been by my own strength. Every step of SME Ally has been sustained by God, and I'm grateful for how far He has brought it.
