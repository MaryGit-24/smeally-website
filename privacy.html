Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026

SME Ally respects your privacy. This notice explains, in plain words, what we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have. We follow Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023.

Who we are

SME Ally is run by Udo Mary Imeabasi in Lagos, Nigeria. You can reach us at ime@smeally.com or on WhatsApp at +234 815 572 0669.

What we collect

When you fill in the "Tell us about your business" form, we collect what you type: your name, business name, kind of business, city and country, WhatsApp number, what is taking your time, and whether you want a call. If you book a call, the booking calendar also collects your email address and the time you choose.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to you and understand your business before we talk.
  • To arrange and remind you about your call.

We do not use it for anything else, and we never sell it.

Who helps us handle it

We use a few trusted services to run this website: Vercel hosts the site, Web3Forms delivers form answers to our email, and Cal.com runs the booking calendar. They only handle your details to provide that service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as we are talking or working together, and for up to two years after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Email ime@smeally.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The booking calendar may use the cookies it needs to work.
